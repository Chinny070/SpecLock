## A real fund-lock defect found and fixed during this audit

Writing a Direct Mode test for the **full** `request_resubmission` cycle (not just the budget-exhaustion revert the existing test suite covered) found that `submit_evidence` never advanced the phase out of `RESUBMISSION_REQUESTED`, so `request_evaluation` (which only accepts `SUBMITTED`/`RE_EVALUATING`) could never be called again — a genuine, previously-untested fund-lock with no escape (since `cancel_or_expire`'s wind-down also only accepted `FROZEN`). A related defect in `_evaluate_api_profile` meant that even after fixing the phase transition, the *point* of a resubmission (corrected evidence) was ignored on re-evaluation, since it always re-probed the first (stale) evidence ever submitted for a criterion rather than the latest. Both are fixed, covered by two new regression tests, and live-verified end to end on a redeployed contract (`0x2671cd7Fc9f7653f8e8A506E4aC3281A1Db0553c`). See `DECISION.md` item 12 and `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` for the full evidence.

## Native escrow and appeal bonds: failed, root-caused, fixed, redeployed, live-verified

Capabilities #3 and #29 reflect genuine engineering work across multiple passes. A first payable escrow design paid out via `gl.get_contract_at(...).emit_transfer(...)`, which failed live against a plain wallet (EOA) with `"Contract ... not found"` — the wrong GenVM primitive for the job, not a runtime bug. The fix, `gl.evm.contract_interface` (an EVM-layer proxy, `_EthAccount`), was found via a sibling project in this account (AgentCourt) with real live proof it works. Two redeploy attempts to verify the fix failed at the constructor-call level due to a malformed `genlayer deploy --args '[]'` CLI invocation (confirmed by comparing raw deploy calldata byte-for-byte; not a platform issue). Corrected, redeployed, and fully live-verified: a real `PASS` settlement with a real provider withdrawal, a real `FAIL`-then-appealed settlement with a real bond correctly credited and withdrawn, and live-tested double-withdrawal and no-balance-withdrawal rejections. Full evidence: `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md` "Native-value escrow: live-verified", `DECISION.md` items 8, 10, 11.
